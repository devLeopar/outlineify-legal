# Delete Your Outlineify Account and Data

Last updated: October 31, 2026

You can delete your Outlineify account and its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app. It works the same way on iPhone, iPad and Android.

## How to delete your account in the app

1. Open Outlineify.
2. Tap **Account** in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap **Delete account and data**.
4. Confirm with **Delete account**.

The app then removes your account and starts again as a new, empty account.

## What is deleted

- Your account: the anonymous identifier the app created when you first opened it, your subscription status as recorded by us and your notification token.
- All your drawings: the finished pages and colored examples in our storage, and the list of your conversions.
- Your uploaded photos. These are already deleted when each conversion finishes, fails or is cancelled, and in any case within one day.
- Your customer record at RevenueCat, the service that tells us whether your subscription is active.
- If you linked Sign in with Apple, that link is revoked.
- Pages the app saved on your device for offline use.

## What is kept

- **Weekly limit record.** To keep the weekly fair-use limit fair, we keep a coded, one-way reference to your subscription together with that week's page count. It cannot be used to identify you, and we erase it automatically about three weeks after that week ends.
- **Free page record.** So that the free first page cannot be claimed again by reinstalling the app or starting a new account, we keep a salted one-way code made from your device identifier the fact that its free page was used and whether it was blocked for abuse, no longer linked to any account. The code cannot be turned back into the device identifier.
- **App usage events.** PostHog keeps them for a limited period under its standard retention settings. They are linked only to your anonymous identifier, so after you delete your account they no longer belong to any account. Email us if you want them deleted.
- **Pages you saved or shared yourself.** Images saved to your photo library or gallery, and PDFs you shared or saved elsewhere, stay where you put them.

## Your subscription is not cancelled

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription. Cancel it where you bought it to stop future charges:

- **App Store (iPhone and iPad):** open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Outlineify.
- **Google Play (Android):** open the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and choose Outlineify.

## If you can't use the app

If you no longer have the app or can't open it, email [agensgratu@gmail.com](mailto:agensgratu@gmail.com) and ask us to delete your account. Because Outlineify does not ask for your name or email, we may ask for details that help us find your account, such as the date of your subscription purchase.
